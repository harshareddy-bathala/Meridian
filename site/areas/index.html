Watch an area

Keep an eye on a place you care about.

Pick an area (a farm, a forest, a town) and follow it over time, from public satellite data: what changed, when, and how sure Meridian is about it.

Not open yet The software is built. It opens once the team has agreed how areas are published.

What you could follow.

  • Fires

    New hot spots inside the area, as satellites report them, with the time each was seen.

  • Crops and forest

    How green the area is, season by season, and when it changes faster than usual.

  • Night lights

    How bright the area is after dark, month by month: towns growing, or power going out.

  • Cloud

    How often the area is clear enough to see, which decides how much the other three can say.

What it will look like.

How it will work.

  • An area is registered by the team, never through a public form, so every area is a decision somebody accountable took. A station owner who wants one asks.
  • Every registered area is public: its name, its rough outline, its figures and the sources each figure came from. A name never describes a person or an address.
  • Publishing is switched on deliberately, area by area, once the rules for it are agreed. Until then this page says so, and shows no real area.

The data comes from public satellite archives, not from Meridian's own stations, and every number is computed from the archive's data itself, never read off a coloured map.
