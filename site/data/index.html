Data and images

Satellite data, and what happened to every pass.

Meridian publishes three things: the weather images its stations receive, the result of every pass it planned, and a dataset that lets anyone check its figures.

Waiting for first light No real station has received anything yet. Nothing on this page is borrowed from another network.

What you will be able to get.

  • Weather images

    Pictures of the Earth decoded from Meteor-M's LRPT signal, after each good pass over a Meridian station.

    After first light

  • Every pass, and its result

    For each pass: whether it was planned, whether the station listened, how usable the data was, and the cause if it was lost.

    Live, practice fleet

  • The evidence dataset

    A sealed copy of the passes, the settings and the seed behind every published figure, so the figures can be rerun. Free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

The network, live.

The dashboard shows every station, every planned pass and every result as it happens. Today it shows a practice fleet of 50 stations Simulated, each marked as simulated. They behave like real stations, faults included, so every step can be seen working before the real one is switched on.

  • Open the live dashboarddash.meridian.org.in. Opens on any phone; nothing to install.↗

For your own programs.

Everything the dashboard shows comes from a public, read-only API, with no key to apply for. Every answer says whether it is simulated or measured, and says so in words when something has not been computed yet, instead of returning a blank.

  • /api/v1/stations: the network's stations
  • /api/v1/passes: upcoming and past passes
  • /api/v1/observations: what each pass returned
  • /api/v1/reliability: how reliable each station has been

The API describes itself, every route and the shape of every answer, at dash.meridian.org.in/openapi.json.

LRPT

The digital picture signal sent by the Meteor-M weather satellites on 137 MHz. It is what Meridian's first station is built to receive.

Every term

Pass

The few minutes a satellite spends above a station's horizon. Each pass happens once. If it is missed, its data is gone.

Evidence dataset

A sealed, downloadable copy of every pass and its result, with the settings used, so anyone can check Meridian's figures for themselves.

Meridian

Open software that decides which satellite passes are worth catching, catches them, checks the result and says what happened.
